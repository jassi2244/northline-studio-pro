Turning education into a credible product.

A responsive website for a stock market education brand, designed to make courses easier to understand, strengthen trainer credibility and give prospective learners clear paths to enquire.

A lot of information, made easier to understand.

Financial education websites can quickly become overloaded with courses, fees, curriculum details, trainer information and disclaimers.

Chart Academy was structured to organise that information into a clearer learning journey while keeping important enquiry actions visible throughout the experience.

01 / Challenge

Explain enough. Overwhelm less.

Prospective learners need enough information to understand what they are buying, but too much content presented at once can make the decision harder rather than easier.

01 / Courses

Make the offer clear

Courses, duration, learning mode and key information are structured so visitors can understand the offering quickly.

02 / Trust

Establish credibility

Trainer information, experience and certification are positioned as part of the decision journey, not hidden as secondary content.

03 / Conversion

Make enquiry simple

WhatsApp contact and clear calls to action reduce friction when a learner is ready to ask questions or discuss a course.

02 / Website direction

Market inspired. Still easy to use.

The visual language uses a dark financial-market atmosphere with chart-inspired elements and green accents, while keeping course information readable and actions easy to identify.

Homepage

The opening section immediately communicates what Chart Academy teaches and gives visitors direct access to course exploration and enquiries.

Courses

Course information is separated into clear sections covering curriculum, duration, learning options and relevant details.

Trust

Trainer background and academy information support credibility before visitors are asked to enquire.

Responsive

Content, navigation and calls to action adapt for mobile screens so the information remains usable without becoming a dense desktop layout squeezed into a smaller viewport.

03 / Conversion flow

Built around the enquiry decision.

The website answers common questions before asking visitors to contact the academy. Course information, trainer credibility, curriculum and FAQs all support the decision to enquire.

WhatsApp access and brochure availability provide practical next steps for visitors who want more information.

Responsive design WhatsApp Brochure flow Course content FAQs
04 / Implementation

Designed to explain. Built to respond.

The project was implemented as a responsive website with structured content and direct lead-generation paths rather than as a simple static course poster online.

Structure

Organised course, curriculum, trainer and FAQ information into a clear page hierarchy.

UI

Created the market-inspired visual direction while maintaining readable typography and clear content separation.

Frontend

Implemented responsive layouts, interactions and enquiry-focused elements across the website.

Lead flow

Integrated WhatsApp actions, brochure access and enquiry opportunities into the content journey.

05 / Ownership

One project. Direct ownership.

Chart Academy was handled independently across structure, visual direction, responsive frontend implementation and enquiry flow.

Direction

Defined the website structure around course discovery, credibility and lead generation.

Presentation

Turned course and trainer information into a more cohesive digital product experience.

Responsive UI

Adapted the interface for desktop and mobile rather than treating responsiveness as an afterthought.

Delivery

Brought content presentation, interface design and lead-generation functionality together as one complete website.
