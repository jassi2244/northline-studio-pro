Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Northline Studio handles information submitted through this website.

Northline Studio is an independent web design and full-stack development practice led by Jaspreet Singh. Information is collected only where reasonably required to respond to enquiries, discuss projects and operate the website.

Information collected

When you submit a project enquiry, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company, country, website, project requirements, budget, expected timeline and other information included in your message.

You are not required to provide information that is not relevant to your enquiry.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Review and respond to project enquiries.
  • Discuss project requirements and technical scope.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals or estimates.
  • Communicate about an existing or potential project.
  • Maintain reasonable business and enquiry records.
  • Protect the website from misuse, spam or abuse.

Sharing of information

Personal information is not sold, rented or provided to advertisers.

Limited information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, such as hosting, email, infrastructure, analytics or security providers. These services may process information only as needed to provide their functionality.

Website analytics

Northline Studio may use privacy-conscious website analytics or performance tools to understand general website usage, traffic sources, device information and technical performance.

Analytics data is used to improve the website and understand how visitors interact with its content.

External links

This website may contain links to external services such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn.

Once you leave the Northline Studio website, the privacy practices of the external service apply. Northline Studio is not responsible for the privacy policies or content of third-party websites.

Data retention

Enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, maintain business records, manage potential projects or meet legal and administrative requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact Northline Studio if you would like to ask about information previously submitted through the website, request a correction or request deletion where reasonably applicable.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, services or supporting technology change.

The latest version published on this page will apply.
